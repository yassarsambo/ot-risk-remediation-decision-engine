# OT-RRDE: product hypothesis and validation notes

**23 November 2026 · Draft research plan · v0.3 development**

These notes retain the detailed planning behind the [practitioner feedback brief](v0.3-product-problem-brief.md). They are intended for methods criticism and study preparation. The linked audit and research protocol include policy behaviour and pilot outputs. Arrange a separate case-only packet before recruiting a scorer whose judgements will be described as blinded.

## User and problem to test

The proposed user is an OT vulnerability analyst preparing a case for an operations or asset owner. A possible sponsor is the OT security lead. Initial discovery should focus on teams that already maintain vulnerability records and reconcile advisories, asset facts and change prerequisites.

**Hypothesis:** reconciling missing, conflicting or late evidence consumes material effort and can produce poorly justified investigation or remediation advice. The frequency, cost and importance of this problem remain unvalidated. Investigate successful existing processes and examples without evidence-reconciliation difficulties as well as problematic ones.

## Proposed smallest useful workflow

For one asset/vulnerability at a fixed decision time, import structured, permitted offline records and produce a reviewable decision note. It should show evidence origins and dates, supported claims, unresolved conflicts, investigation urgency, candidate next steps and outstanding operational prerequisites. Qualified humans retain change authority.

The published v0.2.1 engine provides the research baseline. Development code checks the timestamp consistency and cutoff eligibility of supplied EPSS assertions. It does not authenticate snapshots or establish that the appropriate daily edition was supplied. Broader source-conflict and correction handling remains proposed. A claimed control or approval is an input assertion requiring evidence. Initial evaluation uses synthetic dossiers; arbitrary-document ingestion and the full proposed workflow are not implemented.

## Existing approaches and possible contribution

Manual records, advisory review and change control are discovery comparators whose actual use must be established in interviews. [SSVC][1] already includes mission, safety, exposure and mitigation context. [Tenable][2] documents threat-informed OT prioritisation and remediation insights; [Claroty][3] describes contextual CPS exposure assessment and prioritisation.

**Potential contribution:** make the evidence available at a decision cutoff reproducible and its uncertainty easier to review. Public documentation establishes overlap; it does not establish that competing tools lack these capabilities. Vendor descriptions are not comparative performance evidence. Novelty and comparative benefit remain open.

## Evidence before expanding the product

| Question | Planned evidence |
| --- | --- |
| Is the problem recurring and costly? | Three to five discovery conversations across at least two organisations. Record a recent non-confidential example, current alternatives, task frequency, preparation/review time, rework and decision ownership. These are recruitment targets, not proof of demand. |
| Does the policy improve decisions? | Follow the [validation protocol](v0.3-research-validation-plan.md): two independent scorers; 24 held-out vignettes in 12 case families; frozen cases, policies, adapters and scoring rules. Preserve disagreements and unscorable cases. These are planned study requirements, not completed validation. |
| Is the comparison fair? | Address the [SSVC audit](v0.3-ssvc-pilot-mapping.md) finding of 0/8 complete input combinations through a dated dossier revision. This measures input completeness, not 0/8 correct decisions or evidence of SSVC inferiority. Preserve the [original reviewer packet](v0.3-reviewer-pilot.md) and any ratings made against it. Judge investigation urgency and deployment priority separately; report each endpoint's scope and coverage. |
| Is the workflow useful? | A separately agreed offline pilot comparing the participant's existing process with the proposed workflow. Measure total evidence-entry and review time, clarification requests and acceptable actions; vary method order across participants. Agree an improvement target and acceptable quality trade-offs before the pilot. |

Proposed research gates are zero unsafe proposals on explicit-blocker cases, zero post-cutoff influence on timing challenges and fewer unsupported-evidence decisions than v0.2.1, while preserving its escalation of jointly rated urgent cases. Report routine escalation, unresolved cases, coverage and raw denominators alongside any improvement. Report an endpoint as inconclusive if the relevant cases or agreed judgements are insufficient. Zero observed failures in a small synthetic set does not establish safety or field effectiveness.

## Recruitment and independent scoring

Discovery conversations, methods criticism and case scoring serve different purposes. Record which activity each participant contributes to and agree how the contribution may be used. Obtain separate permission for public naming or quotation; personal feedback is not an employer endorsement.

For scoring, distribute a standalone packet containing only the frozen dossiers, instructions and blank rating form. Record its version, source commit and hash, the delivery date, prior project exposure and when ratings were locked. Keep model outputs, expected labels and pair membership out of that packet. The public repository and comparison workbook expose outputs, so neither is an access-controlled blinded-review environment.

Ask about exposure before scoring and record any later exposure. A participant who has seen outputs can still provide useful methods criticism. Describe their contribution and any effect on the analysis honestly; do not claim their assessment of those cases was blinded. A later held-out study should use fresh case families and a documented distribution procedure. Do not overwrite the original pilot or silently add facts to a rated dossier.

## Next decision

Prepare an interview guide, seek independent criticism and arrange discovery conversations. Expand the workflow only if practitioners independently describe a shared unmet need and a suitable participant agrees measurable pilot objectives. Re-scope if current approaches already solve it or evidence-entry effort outweighs the benefit. Buyer, adoption requirements, pricing and willingness to pay remain unvalidated.

**Neutral opening prompt:** “Walk me through a recent vulnerability decision: what information did you use, how did you reconcile the records, what worked well, what difficulties arose, if any, how much effort did it take, and who approved the next step?”
